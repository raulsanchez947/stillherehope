# CleanKeep Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

CleanKeep is designed to scan files locally on your device and help you review old, junk, important, and security-review files.

## Data We Process

CleanKeep may display local file names, paths, sizes, modification dates, and cleanup categories inside the app. This information is used only to provide scan results and cleanup actions.

## Data We Do Not Collect

CleanKeep does not create an account, upload file contents, sell personal data, track users across apps, or include advertising SDKs in this version.

## Device Permissions

On macOS, CleanKeep asks for access to folders you choose so it can scan and quarantine selected files. On iPhone and iPad, CleanKeep can only access storage made available to the app by iOS.

## Security Review Limitations

CleanKeep may flag risky-looking downloads or executable files for review. These flags are heuristic and do not confirm that a file is malware.

## Data Retention

Scan results are held in app memory while you use the app. Quarantined files remain on your device in `.cleankeep_quarantine` folders until you restore or delete them manually.

## Children

CleanKeep is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy when CleanKeep changes. Material updates should be reflected in the app listing and support site.
