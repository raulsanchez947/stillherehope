Calm companion

An AI chat that listens gently and helps people find a calmer next step.

The guide can work in two modes: a live AI conversation when the server is configured, or a local fallback guide when it is not. In both cases, it is designed to offer grounding, encouragement, practical coping ideas, and clear crisis escalation when needed.

  • Try words like "anxious," "sad," "angry," "alone," or "ground me."
  • Use the quick buttons when words feel hard.
  • If someone may be in danger, use the crisis links below right away.
