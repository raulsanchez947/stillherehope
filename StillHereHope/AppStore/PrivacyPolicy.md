# Still Here Hope Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Still Here Hope is designed to be private, lightweight, and anonymous by default.

## Summary

- No account creation is required
- No login is required
- We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number
- In the current version, your check-ins, saved notes, and written notes are stored locally on your device

## Information We Collect

In the current app configuration, Still Here Hope does not collect personal information from you on our servers.

The app allows you to create and store:

- mood check-ins
- saved notes
- anonymous notes written within the app

This information is stored on your device in the current version of the app.

## How Information Is Used

Your on-device information is used only to make app features work for you, such as:

- showing your mood history
- saving notes you want to keep
- preserving your check-ins between app launches

## No Account System

Still Here Hope does not require account registration. Because there is no account system in the current version, the app does not use personal profile information to identify you.

## Anonymous Notes

Anonymous notes in the current version are not tied to your name or account identity.

## Data Sharing

In the current version, we do not sell your personal data and do not use the app for third-party advertising.

## Crisis and Safety

Still Here Hope is not a substitute for medical, mental health, or crisis care. If you may be in immediate danger or may harm yourself, call emergency services or 988 in the United States.

## Future Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, account features, analytics, or remote processing, this privacy policy and the app’s App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
